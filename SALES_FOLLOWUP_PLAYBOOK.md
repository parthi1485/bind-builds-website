# Bind Builds — Website Lead Follow-up Playbook

This playbook is generated from the same signals used by the website lead-priority model. It is a follow-up aid, not an automatic sales decision. A person should still read the project brief before contacting the lead.

## Priority

Typical signals:
- ready to discuss scope and next steps;
- land purchased / approved drawings / active rebuild;
- nearer construction timeline;
- budget, area or package information already supplied.

First move:
- Call first.
- If unanswered, send the suggested WhatsApp acknowledgement.
- The call is a qualification conversation, not a proposal presentation.

Call objective:
1. Confirm site/locality and current property/approval stage.
2. Confirm users, floors and approximate built-up requirement.
3. Confirm budget direction and finance readiness.
4. Identify family/decision-makers who need to be part of the next meeting.
5. Confirm realistic design/construction timing.
6. Close with one next step: information request, office/site meeting or proposal review.

Do not book a site visit simply because the lead is marked Priority. Project fit still needs to be confirmed.

## Develop

Typical signals:
- genuine project but one or more readiness details are unclear;
- comparing proposals;
- design/site stage is active but timing is not immediate;
- budget, area or plot status is incomplete.

First move:
- Send one personalised WhatsApp message.
- Ask the single missing question surfaced in the lead record.
- Move to a call after the person replies or when the project timing becomes clearer.

Avoid sending a generic brochure dump before answering the lead's main question.

## Nurture

Typical signals:
- just researching;
- plot not finalised;
- exploratory timing;
- little project information supplied.

First move:
- Resource first, not repeated calls.
- Point to the construction cost calculator, Chennai cost guide, process or FAQ depending on the enquiry.
- Keep the contact useful and low pressure.

A Nurture lead can become Priority later. Re-score when the site, intent or timing changes.

## What appears in the existing lead record

The website keeps the current Google Sheet schema unchanged. The **Requirements** field now contains:

1. sales handoff priority + score;
2. recommended next action and readiness signals;
3. lead intent;
4. follow-up mode and suggested timing;
5. follow-up objective;
6. the first question to ask;
7. a concise suggested WhatsApp reply;
8. the visitor's own requirement notes.

This lets the current Google Sheet and email notification carry the sales context without an Apps Script schema migration.

## Guardrails

- Do not tell the client their score or internal priority.
- Do not promise a site visit, proposal or timeline before qualification.
- Do not treat the model as a reason to ignore a lead.
- Do not make repeated calls to early research leads.
- Keep the first message concise and specific to the information they already supplied.
