## Full business description

Bind Builds is an architect-led construction studio in Chennai, bringing architecture, engineering coordination and site execution into one connected project process.

We work with homeowners on new homes, demolition-and-rebuild projects and selected residential construction projects. Our approach focuses on design clarity, coordinated drawings, transparent specifications, project-specific budgeting, professional site supervision and clear communication before and during construction.

Studio discussions are by appointment in Kurinji Nagar, Ramapuram, Chennai, with site visits planned after an initial project-fit conversation.
